# Foal alarm — how to use it

**The alarm's own phone number:** ____________________ *(save it in your phone as "Foal alarm")*

## Fitting it (each time)

1. Switch it **ON**. (If you just switched it off, leave it off for **30 seconds** first.)
2. Clip it to the headcollar of a **standing** horse, hanging freely like a pendant.
3. Wait for the text **"Foal alarm ARMED"** (a few minutes). Don't walk away until it arrives.
   If you instead get *"NOT armed yet"* after half an hour, check it's clipped on and hanging still.

## What the texts mean

| Text | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **FOALING ALARM - lying down & active** | Go to the mare now. |
| **Mare restless - foaling may be starting** | Early warning — keep an eye on her. |
| **Foal alarm OK (armed)…** | Daily "I'm alive" message. **If this stops arriving, the alarm is dead or flat — check it.** |
| **LOW BATTERY** | Charge it today. |
| **Charger connected / Battery full** | Charging progress. |

You can also **text the word `status`** to the alarm's number at any time — it replies (only to you) with battery, signal and whether it's armed.

## Charging (about every 2 days, or when LOW BATTERY arrives)

1. Unclip the box from the headcollar. **Leave the switch ON.**
2. Plug the **supplied black cable** into the socket on the box, and its big flat
   end into any ordinary **USB-A** wall charger (like the one in the kitchen).
   ⚠ Use **only the supplied black cable**. If it doesn't fit a charger, that charger
   is the wrong kind — modern USB-C chargers will *not* charge it. Any old phone
   charger with the rectangular socket is fine; power rating doesn't matter.
3. The little window shows: **green** = charger connected, **orange** = charging,
   **orange goes out** = full (a *"Battery full"* text also arrives). Empty to full ≈ 5–6 hours.
4. Unplug, clip back onto the standing horse, wait for **"Foal alarm ARMED"** again.

## Rules of thumb

- Switching it off? **Wait 30 seconds** before switching it back on.
- The box must **hang freely** — don't strap it tight against anything.
- Nothing inside the box needs touching — please don't open it.
- Battery reads about **90% when full** — that's normal and deliberate (kinder to the battery).
